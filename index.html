NON FUI

BY ROOK

On their gravestones, the Epicureans used to write

Non fui
Fui
Non sum
Non curo

I was not
I was
I am not
I care not

Epicureanism was a school of ancient Greek and Roman philosophy founded by the Greek philosopher Epicurus (341 to 270 BCE), who taught that humans do not survive death.

In this blog, I try to develop my own system of philosophy as a way of life that answers existential, epistemic, and ethical questions. I agree with Epicurus that we humans have only one short life to live. This blog is part of my effort to live it well.
