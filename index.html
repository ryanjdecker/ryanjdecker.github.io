NON FUI

On their gravestones, the Epicureans used to write

Non fui
Fui
Non sum
Non curo

I was not
I was
I am not
I care not

Non fui
Fui

Non curo

I was not

I agree with them that we have but one short life to live. I hope to spend much of it musing, and I will post my musings here.
