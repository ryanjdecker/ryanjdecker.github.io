Atheism

The English word atheist descends from the ancient Greek insult ἄθεος atheos (a- 'without' + theos 'god'), which...

(Whitmarsh 2021).

Atheist appears in English in the 16th century, followed in the 17th century by deist and then theist. Atheism, deism, theism, and agnosticism each appear in English after their -ist forms (Oxford English Dictionary).

Deist descends through French déiste from Latin deus 'god' (OED).
