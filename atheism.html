Atheism

The English stem atheis- descends from the ancient Greek adjective ἄθεος (atheos), literally without god. The word appears first with the meanings god-forsaken or impious and later with the meaning of nonbelief in the gods (Whitmarsh 2021). Latin atheos. Then it was finally borrowed into European vernacular languages in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, when many derivatives also appear (Durkin 2021).

The word atheist appears in English by 1571 and atheism by 1587. English theism appears later in 1678 and deism in 1682.
